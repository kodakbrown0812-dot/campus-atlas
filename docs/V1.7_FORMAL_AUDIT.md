**Audit date:** August 6, 2026

**Audit mode:** documentation-only; no production behavior or canonical record was changed

## 1. Executive decision

Campus Atlas passes **local vision-board acceptance**. The local system has
truthfully demonstrated this causal path:

> Authentic Exact source → canonical events and reasoning → human revision and
> governance → approved case-scoped mechanism → later legitimate task → Need
> Gate and roadway treatment → bounded immutable packet → causal receipt.

The paid receiving-model call is not required for that local decision. The
preserved proof stops honestly at immutable packet compilation.

Campus Atlas fails **production release acceptance** at this time. This is not
a finding that the local engine is architecturally invalid. It means mandatory
hosted durability, hosted isolation, complete authentication, hosted app/API
parity, full mobile/desktop execution, and real-provider or explicitly accepted
provider-blocked release evidence do not exist yet.

Audit totals:

- PASS: 27
- FAIL: 2
- DEFERRED: 6
- NOT TESTED: 2
- Total findings: 37

`DEFERRED` and `NOT TESTED` are not counted as passes.
