# Campus Atlas V1.7 Slice 4 roadways, packets, and receipts

Slice 4 adds the canonical roadway-first reconstruction pipeline beside the
V4.6 compatibility state. It interprets a current task, selects one primary
roadway, discovers and treats project-scoped candidates, compiles a bounded
immutable packet, and atomically saves a causal receipt.

The frozen authority remains
[`V1.7_IMPLEMENTATION_BLUEPRINT.md`](V1.7_IMPLEMENTATION_BLUEPRINT.md).
Slice 4 does not add receiving-model handoff, final packet-diff presentation,
the four-destination navigation redesign, or any Slice 5 or Slice 6 behavior.

## Canonical additions

- The existing `roadways` and `roadway_versions` tables now hold a
  deterministic project-scoped registry for exactly three version-1 roadways:
  Broad Lock-Finding, Margin / Run-Line Value, and Outcome / Postmortem.
- Roadway records have stable IDs. Versions are immutable and include purpose,
  applicable and non-applicable task patterns, required checks, supporting
  modules, required counterevidence, required live state, widening and
  narrowing rules, stop conditions, packet contract, and authority.
- Roadway authority comes from the frozen V1.7 Blueprint. The registry contains
  reasoning checks and structures, not conclusions or Sports Engine picks.
- `live_state_snapshots` stores provider, source identity, category, entity,
  exact raw and normalized values, observed and validity times, freshness
  window, supersession state, conflict group, metadata, and a project-scoped
  idempotency key. Slice 4 adds no broad external integration.
- Packets now preserve the complete structured task interpretation, final
  estimated token count, comparison key, and project-scoped idempotency key.
  Compatibility rows may retain null comparison/idempotency values; all Slice
  4-created packets set both.
- Packet items have a unique sequence within a packet. Receipts have a
  one-to-one packet constraint.
- D1 triggers reject updates and deletes of roadway versions, packets, packet
  items, and receipts. Historical snapshots are append-only.

Packet creation uses one D1 batch for the packet, every packet item, and its
receipt. Failed compilations caused by missing required state or a minimum-safe
packet that cannot fit are also persisted with an honest failed status and
receipt. A failure is never returned as a compiled success.

## Roadway selection

The server records:

- Literal request
- Requested decision or output
- Active project and optional case
- Domain and task/market type
- Time sensitivity
- Broad or narrow scope
- Required reasoning mechanism
- Relevant shared meanings
- Material ambiguity
- Primary roadway and version
- Supporting modules and required live state

Selection uses explicit intent and mechanism families. If two interpretations
could materially change the packet, or the task contains no safe
mechanism-specific signal, compilation returns `clarification_required` and
does not create a packet. A caller may select one of the project’s roadway IDs
or stable slugs for that run. The override is recorded in the interpretation
and never edits the permanent registry.

Every created packet has exactly one primary roadway and version.

## Discovery and ranking

Candidate discovery reads only the requested project and uses separate,
inspectable signals:

- Mechanism-family semantic similarity
- Deterministic keyword/BM25-style term frequency
- Entity overlap
- Temporal fit
- Relationship proximity to the optional case
- Project scope

The current Worker has no embedding provider or search index, so semantic
discovery is an honest mechanism-family approximation rather than a claimed
vector search. The signals discover candidates only. They do not establish
truth, authority, the roadway, treatment, or packet inclusion.

Candidates currently come from canonical mechanisms and versions, reasoning
nodes and versions, findings and versions, events, source artifacts, and
live-state snapshots.

Ranking is lexicographic rather than a universal importance score:

1. Task and mechanism match
2. Scope fit
3. Authority
4. Evidence strength

Tie-breaks use stronger reality contact, more direct mechanism fit, applicable
freshness, independent repetition, lower redundancy, and the shorter
functionally equal representation.

## Use, Consider, and Exclude

Every consequential candidate receives exactly one server-side treatment.

`Use` is limited to:

- A scope-appropriate approved mechanism with direct task/mechanism fit
- A fresh, provider-attributed live-state snapshot that satisfies a roadway
  requirement
- Required checks from the approved roadway contract

`Consider` includes relevant inferred, proposed, under-review, observed, or
challenged material; strong counterevidence; user corrections without durable
governing authority; and reconstructed source artifacts.

`Exclude` includes wrong-mechanism, wrong-scope, rejected, retired, superseded,
stale-current-state, redundant, and budget-displaced records. Every exclusion
retains a concise reason in the packet-item snapshot and receipt.

Approved-local mechanisms require the active case to match a supporting case.
No query reads another project’s candidates. Cross-project content requires a
future explicit governed transfer and is never silently retrieved. Conflicting
approved mechanisms remain `Use` with representation `Conflicted`; both are
recorded as an unresolved conflict instead of being resolved by recency,
frequency, or wording confidence.

## Freshness

Time-sensitive roadways name their required live-state categories. A snapshot
is fresh only while its provider timestamp plus freshness window remains valid
and its canonical status is active. Superseded or stale snapshots cannot enter
`Use`.

Outcome/Postmortem accepts a canonical outcome event as the required reality
contact. Current margin and broad-selection tasks require fresh market
availability, current price, and participant status. Conceptual or historical
tasks do not invent a current-state requirement merely because they use the
same roadway.

Missing state stops compilation when safe reasoning requires it. Timestamped
canonical fixtures exercise the contract; no sports data provider was added.

## Packet budget and compression

Supported budgets are exactly 400, 800 (default), and 1,600 tokens.

The Worker consistently estimates tokens as `ceil(character count / 4)`,
matching the deterministic approximation already used by V4.6. This is not
presented as a receiving-model tokenizer. The final estimate is stored and
enforced against the selected budget.

Compression:

- Removes functionally equal redundancy first
- Uses concise packet wording while the immutable item and receipt retain full
  reasons and metadata
- Preserves required roadway checks
- Preserves Cody corrections
- Preserves unresolved conflicts
- Preserves the strongest applicable counterevidence
- Reclassifies lower-priority optional material as `Exclude` with a budget
  reason
- Fails explicitly when the minimum safe packet cannot fit

The compiler never silently exceeds the selected budget or converts a narrow
task into a project summary.

Every packet item stores source type and ID, source version, treatment,
representation, scope, authority, freshness, reason, and sequence order.
Representation is one of Exact, Compressed, Reconstructed, Inferred, or
Conflicted.

## Receipt and comparison

The receipt preserves:

- Literal request and inferred intent through the packet snapshot
- Selected roadway and selection reason
- Supporting modules
- Alternative roadway interpretations and non-selection reasons
- Full Use, Consider, and Exclude groups and treatment reasons
- Authority, scope, freshness, and inference disclosure
- Governance events that changed eligibility
- Unresolved conflicts
- Token budget and final size
- Prior comparable packet
- Exact item-level changes

Comparable packets share project, optional case, domain, task/market type,
scope, and primary roadway version. The exact comparison reports additions,
removals, and changes to treatment, representation, authority, scope, or
freshness.

When a Slice 3 approval creates an eligible mechanism, the receipt may state
that Cody’s governance event made that version eligible for `Use`. It
explicitly makes no claim that the mechanism caused a correct decision.

## API and interface

All routes are project-scoped under `/api/v1/projects/:projectId`.

- `GET /roadways`
- `POST /reconstruction/interpret`
- `GET|POST /packets`
- `GET /packets/:packetId`
- `GET /packets/:packetId/receipt`
- `GET /packets/:packetId/comparison`
- `POST /live-state`

Writes retain the fail-closed bearer boundary. Packet and live-state creation
require idempotency keys. Generic canonical writes to roadways, roadway
versions, live state, packets, packet items, and receipts are rejected so
callers cannot bypass the domain service.

The temporary project route `/projects/:projectId/ask` provides only the Slice
4 verification surface: submit a task, inspect intent and ambiguity, select a
current-run override, choose 400/800/1,600, inspect Use/Consider/Exclude,
compile, read the packet and receipt, and view prior-packet changes. It does not
replace the V4.6 shell or implement the final navigation.

## Brewers limitation

The user-supplied Brewers artifact remains `Reconstructed`, `observed`, and
non-governing. Any applicable packet item and receipt state that the historical
raw transcript is unavailable and that the artifact is not Exact.

An Exact native Atlas outcome fixture proves the distinct representation path.
Slice 4 does not relabel the reconstruction, invent original messages, claim
the authentic historical import passed, or automatically promote the artifact.
The five historical raw-Brewers tests remain the explicit
`deferred_historical_fixture_gap`.
